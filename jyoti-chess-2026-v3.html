📜 Tournament Rules & Regulations

⚖ General Rules

  • Conducted under FIDE Laws of Chess — Swiss System, 9 Rounds
  • Time Control: 30 min + 30 sec increment per move (Classical)
  • Valid FIDE ID and AICF 2026–27 registration mandatory
  • Foreign players must have valid FIDE ID (FIN)
  • Organisers reserve right to accept or reject any entry

🏅 Tie-Break Order

  • 1. Buchholz Cut 1
  • 2. Buchholz All
  • 3. Sonneborn-Berger (SB)
  • 4. Direct Encounter (DE)
  • 5. Greater number of wins
  • 6. Number of wins with Black pieces

📱 Electronic Devices

  • Mobile phones, tablets strictly prohibited in playing hall
  • All wristwatches (including smart watches) not allowed
  • Earbuds and headphones not permitted during play
  • Players found with such devices will be declared lost

📋 Prize & Protest Rules

  • Prizes will not be shared — one higher prize per player only
  • All winners must attend prize distribution ceremony
  • Protests: submit in writing with ₹2,000 within 10 minutes
  • Protest fee refunded if appeal upheld; else forfeited
  • Organisers' decision is final and binding on all parties

🚪 Withdrawals & Conduct

  • Withdraw by informing Chief Arbiter in writing only
  • Players/parents disrupting decorum will face exclusion
  • No refund under any circumstances once registered
  • Accommodation to be arranged by participants

🎓 AICF Compliance

  • AICF 2026–27 registration is compulsory for all Indians
  • Must be completed before the start of the tournament
  • Online mode only — New: prs.aicf.in/new-register
  • Renewal: prs.aicf.in/players
  • Income Tax Act & Rules applicable on prize money
