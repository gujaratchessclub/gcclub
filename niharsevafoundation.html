Overview

Everything you need to know before you register

  • 01FormatFIDE Laws of Chess, Swiss System, 9 rounds. Tie-breaks: Buchholz Cut 1 → Buchholz All → Sonneborn-Berger → Direct Encounter → Wins → Wins with Black.
  • 02EligibilityOpen to rated and unrated players of any age. AICF registration for 2026–27 is compulsory and must be paid online before the tournament starts.
  • 03Venue comfortThe hall runs on centralised AC throughout, for the comfort of players and the parents waiting with them.
  • 04On the dayFree lunch for the first 200 participants. Additional food is available at the venue on a paid basis, ₹200 for unlimited.
  • 05PayoutsPrize money is transferred within 7–20 working days of submitting the required details. Indian Income Tax rules apply, and each player receives only their single highest prize.

Rules & regulations

Read this before you pack your set

WithdrawalsInform the Chief Arbiter in writing if you need to withdraw mid-event.
Electronic devicesMobiles, tabs, smartwatches, earbuds and headphones are strictly not allowed in the hall — possession alone leads to a loss declaration.
DisciplineAny player or parent disrupting decorum will face exclusion from the venue.
ProtestsSubmit in writing with a ₹2,000 fee within 5 minutes of the incident. Refunded only if the protest is upheld.
EntriesOrganisers reserve the right to accept or reject any entry without assigning a reason.
Appeals committeeA 5-member committee is formed before the tournament starts. Its decision is final and binding on all concerned players.
Prize payoutsIndian Income Tax Act and rules apply. Each player receives one — their higher — prize only. The organiser's decision is final and binding.
AICF registrationCompulsory for 2026–27, and must be completed and paid online before the tournament starts.
