FIDE Ratings Lookup

Match a long list of FIDE IDs against the official FIDE ratings database in one pass instead of opening profile pages one by one. Everything runs in your browser — the player list file never leaves your device.

Step 01

Get the official list

Open FIDE's download page and grab the combined TXT list (Standard + Rapid + Blitz). It's roughly 40 MB and FIDE updates it monthly.

ratings.fide.com/download_lists.phtml →

Step 02

Load it here

Choose the .txt file you downloaded. Parsing 40 MB can take a few seconds — that's normal.

Step 03

Paste your FIDE IDs

One per line, or separated by commas/spaces — either works.

Result
IDNameFedTitleStdRapidBlitzBorn

Runs entirely client-side. Re-upload the list file if you reload this page — nothing is stored.
