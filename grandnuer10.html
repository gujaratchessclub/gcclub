Rules & regulations

 

Format & time control
FIDE Laws of Chess, Swiss system, 9 rounds in both formats.
Rapid: 20 minutes + 5 seconds increment per move.
Blitz: 3 minutes + 2 seconds increment per move.
Walkover time: until flag fall.
Tie-breaks, in order
(i) Buchholz Cut 1, (ii) Buchholz, (iii) Sonneborn-Berger, (iv) Direct encounter, (v) Greater number of wins (including wins by forfeit), (vi) Greater number of wins with Black.
Prizes
Prizes will not be shared. A player is eligible for only one higher cash prize. Age-category prizes are for a player’s own age category only, not a higher one. All prize winners must be present at the prize distribution to claim prizes.
Conduct & appeals
Electronic devices, including any kind of watch, are strictly prohibited in the playing area. A protest against a Chief Arbiter decision must be submitted within 10 minutes of the incident with a ₹2,000 fee. The fee is refunded if the appeal is upheld and forfeited otherwise. Only the player concerned may appeal.
Organisers’ right
The organisers reserve the right to accept or reject any entry without assigning a reason.
