📜 Rules & Regulations
  • Tournament under FIDE Laws of Chess, Swiss System, 9 rounds.
  • Time Control: 30 minutes + 30 seconds increment per move (Classical).
  • Tie-Breaks in order: Buchholz Cut 1 → Buchholz All → Sonneborn-Berger → Direct Encounter → Greater number of wins → Wins with Black.
  • Prizes will not be shared — one player eligible for only one higher cash prize. Age-category winners eligible only for their respective age category.
  • All prize winners must be present at the prize distribution ceremony to claim prizes.
  • Organisers reserve the right to accept/reject any entry without assigning reason.
  • Withdrawals: Must inform the Chief Arbiter in writing.
  • Electronic Devices: Mobiles, tabs, smartwatches, earbuds & headphones are strictly not allowed in the hall — possession leads to a loss declaration.
  • Discipline: Any player/parent disrupting decorum will face exclusion.
  • Eligibility: Open to all rated & unrated players. Foreign players require a valid FIDE ID (FIN).
  • Protests: Must be submitted in writing with a fee of ₹2,000/- within 10 minutes of the incident. Refunded only if upheld.
  • AICF Registration 2026–27 is compulsory and must be paid online before the tournament starts.
  • All prize money will be transferred within 7–20 working days after submission of required details.
  • Organizer's decision will be final & binding.
